# ScamShield – Online Scam Awareness Platform

> **"Stay Alert. Stay Safe. Stop Online Scams."**

**ScamShield** is a modern, responsive, cybersecurity-themed educational web platform designed to help students, senior citizens, and the general public identify, prevent, and report digital fraud and cybercrimes.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. Hero & Cyber Threat Dashboard
- **Catchy Title & Branding:** *"Stay Alert. Stay Safe. Stop Online Scams."*
- **Live Threat Monitor:** Real-time simulated threat alert card with cybersecurity indicators.
- **Key Fraud Statistics:**
  - **₹1,750+ Cr** saved and frozen by the 1930 helpline.
  - **12 Lakh+** cyber complaints registered annually on the national portal.
  - **78%** of reported scams involve UPI, OTPs, or financial payment breaches.
  - **90%** of frauds are completely preventable with digital hygiene.
- **Fast-Action Navigation Buttons:** Explore Scam Types, Take Safety Quiz, and Emergency Helpline.

### 2. Common Online Scams Decoded (8 Categories)
Every scam card provides an intuitive summary, realistic scenario quote, red flags checklist, and prevention advice:
1. **Phishing Emails & SMS (Smishing)** – Fake bank KYC and electricity bill cut-off threats.
2. **UPI Payment Scams** – Misleading QR codes and false "Collect" requests (OLX buyer scam).
3. **OTP & SIM Swap Fraud** – Impersonation of telecom/bank staff demanding OTPs or screen-sharing tools (AnyDesk/TeamViewer).
4. **Fake Job Offers & Task Scams** – "Earn ₹3,000/day by liking YouTube videos" leading to prepaid VIP deposits.
5. **Online Shopping Scams** – Counterfeit social media shops offering 90% discount on luxury electronics.
6. **Social Media & Impersonation Scams** – Cloned accounts asking for urgent medical transfers via DM.
7. **Lottery & Prize Scams** – WhatsApp notices claiming ₹25 Lakhs KBC winnings requiring advance GST payments.
8. **Fake Investment & Stock Tips** – Telegram groups guaranteeing 20% weekly returns using personal bank transfers.
- **Interactive Filter Tabs:** Filter scams by *Payment & Banking*, *Impersonation & Social*, or *Jobs & Investments*.

### 3. Interactive Scam Awareness Quiz
- **10 Scenario-Based Questions:** Real-life dilemmas covering UPI PIN rules, phishing URLs, remote apps, job scams, 2FA, and the Golden Hour.
- **Instant Visual Feedback:** Option turns green (correct) or red (incorrect), with complete reasoning provided.
- **Dynamic Scoring & Defender Badges:**
  - 9–10: 🛡️ *Cyber Shield Guardian* (Expert)
  - 7–8: ✅ *Scam Aware Defender* (Strong)
  - 5–6: ⚠️ *Cyber Apprentice* (Moderate Risk)
  - <5: 🚨 *High Vulnerability Netizen* (Urgent Attention)
- **Review Mode:** Complete answer breakdown showing question, user's choice, correct choice, and explanation.
- **Retake Feature:** Instant reset to test knowledge again.

### 4. Proactive Cyber Safety Tips
- **6 Core Defense Rules:**
  1. Never share OTPs or passwords under any circumstances.
  2. Avoid suspicious links and shortened domains in SMS/WhatsApp.
  3. Verify payment requests (UPI PIN is ONLY for paying out, never receiving).
  4. Use strong, unique passwords with passphrases.
  5. Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) with Authenticator apps.
  6. Check website URLs for valid HTTPS and accurate domain spellings.
- **Interactive Cyber Hygiene Checklist:** 6-point checklist with dynamic defense meter calculating the user's readiness level.

### 5. Report a Scam (Community Incident Log)
- **Simple, Safe Form:**
  - Scam Category dropdown
  - Platform/Channel (WhatsApp, SMS, Phone Call, Telegram, Instagram, etc.)
  - Suspect Info (Phone Number, Fake Link, or UPI ID)
  - Date of Incident
  - Detailed Description (minimum 20 characters)
  - Optional Reporter Contact Details (supports anonymous reporting)
- **Strict Privacy Guarantee:** Explicitly informs users **NEVER to share** bank accounts, card CVVs, OTPs, or passwords.
- **Client-Side Validation:** Highlighting missing or short fields with actionable error messages.
- **Submission Feedback:** Modal dialog displaying an awareness Reference Code (`#SS-2026-XXXX`) and direct instructions on filing formal police complaints.
- **Recent Community Reports Feed:** LocalStorage-powered feed showing latest anonymized reports.

### 6. Emergency Help & Indian Cybercrime Information
- **National Cybercrime Helpline: 1930** (One-click direct call `tel:1930`).
- **Official National Portal:** [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in).
- **The Golden Hour Protocol:** Explains why reporting within the first 2–3 hours is critical for freezing scammed funds before withdrawal.
- **4-Step Rapid Incident Response:**
  1. Block Cards & UPI immediately in banking app.
  2. Call 1930 Helpline with transaction reference.
  3. Preserve digital evidence (screenshots, SMS, chats).
  4. Register formal e-FIR on `cybercrime.gov.in`.
- **Secondary Helplines:** RBI CMS Portal (`cms.rbi.org.in`), Sanchar Saathi (`sancharsaathi.gov.in`), and National Emergency `112`.

### 7. Professional Footer
- Project overview, quick navigation, scam category directory, 24x7 emergency contacts, and clear educational disclaimers.
