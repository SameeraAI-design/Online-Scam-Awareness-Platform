Know Your Adversaries

Common Online Scams Decoded

Fraudsters exploit human trust, urgency, fear, and greed. Understand the anatomy of the 8 most widespread digital frauds in India and worldwide.

High Risk
Deceptive Messaging

Phishing Emails & SMS

Fraudulent emails, SMS (Smishing), or WhatsApp messages pretending to be from your bank, Income Tax Dept, or courier services (India Post, FedEx) requesting immediate link clicks.

Realistic Scenario:

"Dear Customer, your SBI/HDFC net banking access is blocked due to pending KYC. Click http://bit.ly/sbi-kyc-update immediately to avoid a ₹5,000 penalty."

Red Flags:

  • Urgent threats: "Account suspended in 24 hours"
  • Shortened links (bit.ly, tinyurl) or misspelled domains
  • Asks for password, ATM PIN, or card CVV
How to Protect: Never click links in SMS. Always open your bank's verified official app or type the official URL manually.
Most Frequent
UPI & Wallets

UPI Payment Scams

Scammers trick victims into scanning QR codes or approving "Collect" requests under the false pretense of sending advance payments for goods, refunds, or OLX sales.

Realistic Scenario:

An OLX buyer says: "I want your old sofa for ₹10,000. I have sent a QR code on WhatsApp. Scan it and enter your 6-digit UPI PIN to receive the money in your account."

Red Flags:

  • Claiming you need to enter UPI PIN to receive money
  • Fake screenshots of "Pending payment received"
  • Pushing you to accept a "Collect Money" notification
How to Protect: Golden Rule: UPI PIN is ONLY required to DEBIT money from your account. You never enter a PIN to receive funds.
Critical Risk
Authentication Breach

OTP & SIM Swap Fraud

Scammers pose as bank customer support, telecom executives (5G upgrade), or power department officials asking you to share the OTP sent to your phone or install screen-sharing apps.

Realistic Scenario:

"Hello, I am calling from Airtel/Jio backend. Your 4G SIM will be deactivated tonight. Read out the 6-digit upgrade code sent to your phone to switch to 5G uninterrupted."

Red Flags:

  • Caller requesting OTP under any emergency justification
  • Asking to install apps like AnyDesk, TeamViewer, or RustDesk
  • Instruction to send an SMS like "SIMREQ <Number>" to 121
How to Protect: No genuine bank or telecom employee will ever ask for your OTP. Never share screen access with unknown callers.
High Risk
Employment Fraud

Fake Job Offers & Task Scams

Luring college students and job seekers with "Part-Time Work From Home" promises: like YouTube videos, rate Google Maps hotels, or do data entry for ₹3,000–₹5,000 daily earnings.

Realistic Scenario:

"Earn ₹3,500 daily by simply subscribing to 5 YouTube channels. Initial ₹300 paid to earn trust, followed by Telegram VIP groups asking ₹10,000 to unlock 'prepaid tasks'."

Red Flags:

  • Recruitment conducted entirely over WhatsApp or Telegram
  • Requires paying a "security deposit", "registration fee", or "task fee"
  • Unrealistically high hourly wages for zero qualification
How to Protect: Legitimate companies pay you for your work; they never ask you to pay them money to get or continue a job.
Rampant
E-Commerce

Online Shopping Scams

Counterfeit shopping websites advertised heavily on Instagram and Facebook offering branded footwear, gadgets, or apparel at 80% to 90% discount, delivering nothing or empty boxes.

Realistic Scenario:

"Festive Grand Sale! iPhone 15 Pro for only ₹19,999. First 50 customers only. Pay now via UPI/GPay. Cash on Delivery unavailable due to clearance stock policy."

Red Flags:

  • Absence of physical address, GST number, or customer care helpline
  • Prepaid-only checkout with disabled Cash on Delivery
  • Comments and reviews turned off or disabled on social media ads
How to Protect: Always inspect domain registration age, prefer trusted e-commerce platforms, and beware of deals that seem impossibly cheap.
High Risk
Social Engineering

Social Media & Impersonation

Cloned accounts or compromised profiles of your friends, relatives, or colleagues sending urgent distress messages begging for emergency money transfers or hospital fees.

Realistic Scenario:

An Instagram DM from your school friend's exact name & photo: "Bro, my mother is in ICU and UPI is locked on my phone. Can you GPay ₹15,000 to this hospital medical store QR right now?"

Red Flags:

  • Sudden financial distress request via direct messages
  • Excuses why they cannot speak via voice or video call
  • Account was created recently with few followers
How to Protect: Always disconnect and place a direct phone or video call to that person on their verified mobile number before transferring money.
Common
Prize Fraud

Lottery & Lucky Draw Scams

Messages or WhatsApp voice recordings claiming your mobile number or email won ₹25 Lakhs to ₹1 Crore in KBC (Kaun Banega Crorepati) or luxury car lucky draws.

Realistic Scenario:

"KBC Head Office WhatsApp Notice: Congratulations! Your SIM has won ₹25,00,000. Contact Manager Rana Pratap. Deposit ₹12,500 government GST clearance fee to release funds."

Red Flags:

  • Winning a lottery for a contest you never bought tickets for
  • Demands to pay "processing fee", "tax", or "custom clearance" upfront
  • Fake letterheads with mismatched government logos
How to Protect: You can never win a contest you never participated in. Genuine prizes deduct taxes at source and never demand advance fees.
Massive Losses
Financial Scam

Fake Investment & Stock Tips

Exclusive WhatsApp & Telegram groups claiming to offer SEBI-registered institutional stock picks, pre-IPO allotment shares, or crypto bots promising 200% to 500% guaranteed returns.

Realistic Scenario:

"Join our VIP Institutional Trading Desk. Guaranteed 20% weekly returns on pre-IPO allotment. Download our customized trading APK and transfer investment to our partner current accounts."

Red Flags:

  • Words like "100% Guaranteed Profit" or "Zero Risk"
  • Transferring funds to individual savings accounts rather than registered brokers
  • Fake app shows huge fake balances, but withdrawals are blocked demanding "withdrawal tax"
How to Protect: Invest only through SEBI-registered brokers. Never trade via third-party APKs or Telegram groups.

Proactive Defense

Essential Online Safety Tips

Adopt these 6 golden digital hygiene practices. Practicing them consistently eliminates over 90% of online fraud risks.

01

Never Share OTPs or Passwords

Your One-Time Password (OTP) and ATM/UPI PIN are final approval keys. No legitimate bank, police officer, or company representative will ever ask for them.

Treat OTP as confidential as your cash wallet Never read out OTP to callers claiming KYC updates
02

Avoid Suspicious Links

Phishing messages often arrive via SMS or WhatsApp with short links (bit.ly, is.gd) or fake domains resembling banks or electricity boards.

Type official portal web addresses directly Never download APK files sent via WhatsApp or Telegram
03

Verify All Payment Requests

Scanning a QR code or entering your UPI PIN ALWAYS transfers money away from your account. Receiving money never requires a PIN.

Double-check receiver name before tapping confirm Never accept UPI "Collect Requests" from strangers
04

Use Strong & Unique Passwords

Using your birth year or `Password@123` across all websites makes account takeover trivial once any single service suffers a breach.

Use 12+ character passphrases with symbols Never reuse your netbanking password for social media
05

Enable Two-Factor Auth (2FA)

Activate 2FA across Google, Apple, WhatsApp, and financial portals. Prefer authenticator apps (Google Authenticator, Bitwarden) over SMS.

Turn on WhatsApp 2-Step Verification PIN immediately Don't approve push-authenticator prompts you didn't trigger
06

Check URLs Before Making Payments

Examine the domain suffix carefully. Fraudsters create lookalikes such as `sbi-kyc-banking.online` or `amazon-clearance-deal.shop`.

Confirm HTTPS padlock and exact domain spelling Never input card details on unverified checkout portals

4-Step Rapid Response Protocol

1

Block Cards & Accounts

Immediately open your bank app or call customer support to freeze the affected bank account, UPI ID, and cards.

2

Call 1930 Immediately

Provide the operator with the transaction ID, account number, debit amount, and scammer details to trigger bank freeze.

3

Preserve Digital Evidence

Take screenshots of SMS alerts, UPI transaction history, WhatsApp chats, call logs, and fake website URLs.

4

Register Formal FIR

Visit cybercrime.gov.in to file an official complaint. Note down the acknowledgement number for court & bank recovery.
